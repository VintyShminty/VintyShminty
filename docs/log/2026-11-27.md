## Late night, Telegram alerts for sign ups and log ins

Branch: `feat/telegram-alerts`, merged into `main` as #182

### What was done
- Eden now gets a Telegram message from his own bot for every new account and every log in, from the app and the website alike. A sign up reads "🆕 New sign up: username (Google)", a log in reads "🔑 Log in: username (email and password)".
- Only the username and how they signed in (email and password, email code, Google or Apple) go to Telegram, never an email address.
- No message for the fake stand-in sellers from the admin pages, an admin viewing as someone else, a password change that logs out other devices, or the log in that comes with a sign up (within 2 minutes).
- Telegram being slow or down never slows or breaks a log in. The message goes out after the answer, gives up after 5 seconds, and a refusal is noted in the server log with Telegram's reason.
- Eden made the bot in BotFather and added its two private settings to the live server in Vercel himself (Production only), so his laptop, test copies and tests send nothing. Their names are in `apps/server/.env.example`.
- Confirmed working on 2026-11-28: a real Google log in on the live server arrived in Telegram.

### Decisions
- Eden chose username only, no email address in the alerts (it would send people's personal details to an outside company).
- No "app or website" label. For Google and Apple log ins the server cannot reliably tell which one was used, so it is left out rather than guessed.

### Problems and fixes
- Vercel in Chrome showed "404". Root cause: Chrome was signed in to Eden's personal Vercel account, the server lives in the Vinty account. Fix: Eden signed in to the Vinty account. Lesson: the live server's Vercel project is only visible from the Vinty account.
- First version sent the sign up alert when the person was saved, before their Google or Apple link existed, so a Google sign up could have read "email and password". Fix: the alert now goes out when the first sign in method is saved.
- A password change with "log out other devices" also makes a new login behind the scenes and would have read as a log in. Fix: only real log in addresses count. Covered by a test.
