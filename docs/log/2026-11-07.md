## Income forecast for the business plan

### What was done
- A forecast page with sliders, private to Eden until shared: https://claude.ai/artifact/JTDAWj31tvfukou1W58z6U. It is not in the repo. The page file is kept outside the project, so if the link is ever lost the page has to be made again from this entry.
- The page walks down a chain: people signed up, how many open the app in a month, how many of those buy, orders per buyer, the average price of a sold item, and the buyer protection fee (percent plus an optional fixed amount per order). It shows the orders, the money that changes hands between buyers and sellers, and what Yadii keeps, per month and per year.
- A price mix table builds the average price from kinds of items and their shares, with a button that sends the result into the forecast.
- A bar chart shows fee income at 1,000, 2,000, 5,000, 10,000, 20,000 and 50,000 people with the same assumptions. The people slider goes from 500 to 50,000.
- Real prices from the live site (read through the public catalog on 7 November 2026, the same way the website reads it): 246 listings from 10 sellers. 89 are books, almost all at 10 shekels. The 157 fashion items have a middle asking price of 50 shekels and an average of 66, with half of them between 30 and 80 shekels and only 4 at 200 or more. By kind: women's tops about 40, dresses and jackets about 60, trousers and skirts about 35, women's shoes about 80 (about 3 in 10 fashion listings), men's clothing about 60, bags about 170.
- The forecast's default average price is now 60 shekels, built from that mix. The starting guesses for the rest: 40 percent open the app in a month, 15 percent of those buy, 1.5 orders each, 5 percent fee with nothing fixed. That gives about 270 shekels a month at 1,000 people, 1,350 at 5,000 and 13,500 at 50,000.

### Decisions
- None. The fee is still the placeholder in `docs/roadmap.md` (5 percent, nothing fixed).

### Problems and fixes
- Reading prices straight from the live database was blocked by the safety rules for production reads. Fix: read the same listings through the public catalog address the website uses, which anyone can open. Lesson: for read-only questions about live data, the public catalog is enough and needs no special access.
- The in-app browser could not show the page for a visual check: the published page asks for a Claude sign in, and a copy opened from the disk cannot be captured. The maths and the page's script were checked by running them on their own instead.

### Open questions
- Add a fixed amount per order to the fee, the way Vinted adds about 0.70 euro? At today's prices 3 shekels per order roughly doubles the income. Suggested default: yes, a small fixed part, decided together with the final percent when payments are built.
- Keep books as a category? A 5 percent fee on a 10 shekel book is 50 agorot, and books are a third of the listings today. Suggested default: keep them for now, they bring people in, and decide on the fee per category later.
- These are asking prices. Sold prices will be lower after offers and price drops. Suggested default: plan with 50 to 55 shekels until the first 100 real sales give a measured number.
