## Name check: "Yadii" in Israel (afternoon)

### What was done

- **Searched Israel's official trademark register** (the government list of protected brand names) for the candidate app name "Yadii". Nobody owns "YADI" in English or a live "ידי" in Hebrew anywhere near shopping or apps.
- **Found the one real neighbour: Yad2.** Its company, Coral-Tell, owns "יד2" for online selling and advertising (the same category we would be in) until 2036. They also once filed "yad1", which has since expired. Someone else has "yad-il" under review in the same category.
- **Compared "ידי" with the spelling "יאדי".** "ידי" looks like "יד" plus one letter, just like "יד2", and means "my hand", which points at "יד שנייה" (second hand). "יאדי" breaks that look, reads as an invented word, and nothing is filed under it. Spoken aloud, all of them sound different from "Yad Shtayim".

### Decisions

None. "Yadii" with the Hebrew spelling "יאדי" is a candidate, not chosen. The name is still open item G10 in `docs/originality.md`.
