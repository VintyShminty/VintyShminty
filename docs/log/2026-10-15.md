## Website item page and a calmer top bar (session that began late on 2026-10-14)

Branch: `fix/web-listing-page`, then `feat/web-top-bar`, ending on `main`

### What was done

- **Item pages open at the top.** After scrolling Home and clicking an item, the page used to open still scrolled down with its top cut off. Now it always starts at the top.
- **Card photos open the item.** Before, only the words under a card worked as a link.
- **Hover arrows on cards.** On a laptop, a card with more than one photo shows an arrow on hover to flip through up to 5 photos without leaving Home. The server now sends those photos with each card.
- **Quieter item page.** The price looks like the card price, both cream boxes are gone, the main photo fills a 3:4 frame with no cream bars, and the description sits on the right under the price.
- **Honest Coverage note.** It now says the buyer is protected by Coverage and we step in if the item does not arrive or is not as described, instead of promising the money comes back by itself. English and Hebrew, shared with the app.
- **Three top bar directions mocked up** in `docs/mockups/top-bar-options.html`. Eden picked C and asked for a fully rounded search bar.

- Both pieces are merged and live (#70, #72).

- Item page and card changes, decided by Eden: D-040 in `docs/design.md`.
- Top of the website, direction C with a round search bar, slimmer buttons and pill filters, decided by Eden: D-039 in `docs/design.md`.
- The item page's right panel no longer stays pinned while scrolling (my call, so a long description never hides the seller box). Listed in D-040, Eden can ask for it back.

### Problems and fixes

- **Item page opened scrolled down.** Root cause: the grey placeholder shown while an item loads stopped the website from jumping back to the top. Taking the placeholder away fixed it but made clicks feel slow, so the placeholder now jumps to the top by itself.
- **Card photos were not clickable.** The photo sat on top of the card's invisible link and caught the click. The photo now lets clicks through, while the bookmark and arrows stay their own buttons.
- **"I don't see anything different."** The second branch started from the live version, so it did not have the first branch's listing fixes. Once both were merged, the live site had both. Lesson: when two branches are reviewed one after the other, say which branch the local website is showing.

- **Local website showed empty cards.** The local website had stopped, and after a restart its items stayed blank because Docker (the app that runs the local database) was not running. Starting Docker from here timed out. Eden parked it.

## First Android check and its fixes (afternoon session)

Branch: `fix/android-qa`

### What was done

- First look at the app on Android (the Pixel 10 emulator), first signed out, then on Eden's live account, then on a test copy that talks to the server on the Mac.
- Fixed what it found, all in PR #82, checked by hand on the emulator in Hebrew:
  - The Android back button closes only an open slide up list, everywhere. On the sell form it now asks "save or discard?" like the close cross, instead of closing and losing what was typed.
  - The highlight behind the active tab is a rounded pill on every tab (it was a square, on iPhone too). On Android the bar now sits above the phone's bottom gesture line.
  - "Add photos" opens our own slide up list with Library and Camera instead of the plain Android popup.
  - Listing photos swipe in Hebrew on Android (they were stuck), and the counter reads "1 / 3", not "3 / 1".
  - Round icon buttons flash a round grey when pressed, not a square.
- New working rule: every visible change is checked on both iPhone and Android before its PR, and the PR says which phones were checked. In `docs/playbook/how-we-work.md` (Check both phones) and `CLAUDE.md`.
- An Android test copy of the app (a development build, it gets its code from the Mac) is now on the emulator. It replaced the copy that pointed at the live server.
- Started a separate session for the next step: a small admin section in the app for batch photo uploads, where one batch of photos becomes one listing.

### Decisions

- One codebase stays the rule, there is no separate Android version. Differences are caught by checking both phones (Eden).
- Left as is on purpose: a sold item stays in the home feed marked Sold until the feed reloads (by design, D-032), the tab bar stays slightly see through (same on iPhone), Home stays on the left in Hebrew (D-016).
- The "save or discard?" question still uses the plain system popup. It is used in about 20 places, so restyling it is its own change.

### Problems and fixes

- **The first QA agent tested the wrong app.** It installed Expo Go (a generic preview app) instead of using the real app already on the emulator, and Expo Go crashes on our notification code. Lesson: check which apps are installed before testing.
- **"I signed in" came from a reply button, not from Eden.** The agent was told to continue when nobody had signed in. Stopped it as soon as Eden said so. Lesson: check the screen before acting on a button reply.
- **The test users do not exist online.** The installed app talked to the live server, so they could not log in. A local test copy fixed that.
- **The emulator kept freezing ("System UI isn't responding").** Two causes: the Mac was overloaded (Backblaze backup at full speed, plus the 18 minute build), and Android's own short range radio service (UWB) crashed over 2,700 times in a loop. Fix: closing heavy apps and starting the emulator with that feature off (`-feature -Uwb`). Starting it from Android Studio brings the radio back.
- **The test copy could not download its code** through the emulator's usual link to the Mac (the connection dropped halfway). Pointing it at 127.0.0.1 through adb (the cable like link between Mac and emulator) worked.
- **The square tab highlight took three tries.** A switched class, then an inline style, both still square. Root cause: Android drops a box with no visible background as an optimisation and redraws it later without rounded corners. Fix: tell Android never to drop that box.
- **Photos would not swipe in Hebrew on Android.** Forcing left to right directly on the photo list stops it scrolling on Android. Moving that rule to a box around the list fixed it.
