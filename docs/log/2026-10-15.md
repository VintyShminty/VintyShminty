## Website item page and a calmer top bar (session that began late on 2026-10-14)

Branch: `fix/web-listing-page`, then `feat/web-top-bar`, ending on `main`

### What was done

- **Item pages open at the top.** After scrolling Home and clicking an item, the page used to open still scrolled down with its top cut off. Now it always starts at the top.
- **Card photos open the item.** Before, only the words under a card worked as a link.
- **Hover arrows on cards.** On a laptop, a card with more than one photo shows an arrow on hover to flip through up to 5 photos without leaving Home. The server now sends those photos with each card.
- **Quieter item page.** The price looks like the card price, both cream boxes are gone, the main photo fills a 3:4 frame with no cream bars, and the description sits on the right under the price.
- **Honest Coverage note.** It now says the buyer is protected by Coverage and we step in if the item does not arrive or is not as described, instead of promising the money comes back by itself. English and Hebrew, shared with the app.
- **Three top bar directions mocked up** in `docs/mockups/top-bar-options.html`. Eden picked C and asked for a fully rounded search bar.

- Both pieces are merged and live (#70, #72).

### Decisions

- Item page and card changes, decided by Eden: D-040 in `docs/design.md`.
- Top of the website, direction C with a round search bar, slimmer buttons and pill filters, decided by Eden: D-039 in `docs/design.md`.
- The item page's right panel no longer stays pinned while scrolling (my call, so a long description never hides the seller box). Listed in D-040, Eden can ask for it back.

### Problems and fixes

- **Item page opened scrolled down.** Root cause: the grey placeholder shown while an item loads stopped the website from jumping back to the top. Taking the placeholder away fixed it but made clicks feel slow, so the placeholder now jumps to the top by itself.
- **Card photos were not clickable.** The photo sat on top of the card's invisible link and caught the click. The photo now lets clicks through, while the bookmark and arrows stay their own buttons.
- **"I don't see anything different."** The second branch started from the live version, so it did not have the first branch's listing fixes. Once both were merged, the live site had both. Lesson: when two branches are reviewed one after the other, say which branch the local website is showing.

- **Local website showed empty cards.** The local website had stopped, and after a restart its items stayed blank because Docker (the app that runs the local database) was not running. Starting Docker from here timed out. Eden parked it.
