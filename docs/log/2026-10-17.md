## Morning: the bundle bar in the app

### What was done

- The brown bundle bar at the bottom of an item page is now a rounded espresso pill floating just above the tab bar. It has the same height, rounded shape and soft shadow as the tab bar, and sits high enough to clear the round Sell button. Before, it was a flat brown block running from the bottom of the screen up behind the tab bar.
- The pill shows only after you tap "+ Bundle" or "Add to bundle", and goes away when the bundle is empty. An old bundle no longer makes it appear on its own. The app forgets this when it is closed.
- You can pick more items for a bundle again. The "More from {seller}" row on the item page, where each card has its "+ Bundle" button, had squashed to zero height, so only its heading showed.
- Adding and removing feel smooth now:
  - The bundle box, the step tiles, the "+ Bundle" buttons and the pill all change the moment you tap. The server catches up behind them (an "optimistic" change: the screen shows the expected result before the server confirms it).
  - The box grows and shrinks over about a quarter of a second, and the page under it glides along.
  - Lit step tiles fade in, and the pill rises in and sinks away.
  - All of this is skipped with Reduce Motion on.
- In the "this item is in your bundle" state, View bundle and Remove are now stacked instead of side by side, so the box keeps the same height when you add.
- The pill's top line reads "2 items, 5% off", and becomes "2 items · You save ₪37" once the server has the prices. It fits one line in Hebrew.
- Checked in the iOS Simulator in Hebrew against a local server. The motion was checked from screen recordings, frame by frame.
- Pull request #109 is open, waiting for Eden's merge. Auto-fix is on, so a failing check or a review comment brings this session back to fix it.

### Decisions

- Eden: the pill appears only after an add tap, and disappears when the bundle is emptied. Recorded as D-050 in `docs/design.md`.
- To stop the page jumping, the "in your bundle" buttons are stacked (View bundle over Remove). Also in D-050.
- The website keeps the old bar for now. Whether it should follow is an open question in #109.

### Problems and fixes

- The bundle work had already been merged into `main` (#107), but this folder's branch was older. The work moved to a fresh copy cut from the latest `main` so the unsaved files in the main folder stayed untouched.
- "Can't pick more items": the cards in the seller's row fill their space by stretching, and inside a sideways scrolling row with no set height they stretched to nothing. Laying each card's holder out as a row lets the card take its own height.
- The local server running in the main folder was older and did not know about bundles. A second server was started from the fix copy on another port. Port 3010 was already taken by another copy's server, so it moved to 3020. The Simulator's app build only looks for its app bundler on the standard port, so that ran on 8081.
- The first pill cut off its Hebrew saving and almost touched the Sell button. The saving line was allowed to wrap and the pill was raised. That wrap was later dropped when the saving took the percent's place on one line.
- Smoothing took three rounds, each checked from a screen recording frame by frame:
  1. A fade on the box and a slide on the pill. Eden: still jumpy. The recording showed three things: about 0.2 seconds of waiting for the server with the buttons greyed out, the box going blank before fading back in, and the page snapping to the new height.
  2. The box switched at the tap, changed in place without blanking, and grew smoothly. Eden: better, but still laggy. The recording showed the pill arriving about 0.2 seconds after the box, and the resize starting slowly.
  3. The pill now moves with the tap too, and the resize starts quickly and settles softly. Everything starts about 60ms after the tap and finishes together.
- Lesson: for motion, record the screen and read the frames with their timings. Single screenshots are too slow to show where it jumps.
