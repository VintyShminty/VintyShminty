Not yet checked on the iPhone.

## Decisions

- Eden: move the live database from Neon Free to Launch. It costs $0.106 per hour of database time at full size, our database runs at a quarter of that size, so about $13 a month at the current pace and about $19 if it never sleeps. Storage ($0.35 per GB a month) is under a cent for our 8 MB. Recorded in `docs/knowledge/decisions.md` (Product and data), and the 2026-10-18 "stay on the free plan" line is marked superseded.
- Not chosen: staying free by cutting what wakes the database (it would come back as soon as real people use the app), and moving to another Postgres host (cheaper by a few dollars, but means moving the data and changing addresses).

## Problems and fixes

- **App dead, "Something went wrong" everywhere.** Root cause: the free plan gives 100 hours of database time a month. The database falls asleep after 5 quiet minutes, but testing, the website, the app's small activity reports and search engine visits wake it every few minutes, so it was awake far more than 3 hours a day. The 2026-10-18 entry already saw it heading for about 117 hours. The data drain fix from that day (#133) solved the other limit, data sent out, not this one. Fix: the paid plan, which has no hour cap. Lesson: on the free plan the hour cap, not the data, is what runs out first for us.
- **Why Neon at all (Eden asked).** Neon is Postgres, the same database we run on the Mac in Docker, rented on a computer that is always online. It was picked on 2026-10-13 because it plugs straight into Vercel, had a free plan and sits in Frankfurt next to the server.
