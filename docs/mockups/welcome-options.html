The welcome screen, three directions

Each direction shows the welcome screen, then Log in and Sign up in the same style, in English and in Hebrew (right to left). Tell me the number you like: 1, 2 or 3. Any headline from the strip below can go with any direction, so you pick the look and the words separately.

All three fix what was wrong: no stranded logo and empty gap, one weight in the headline (the thin plus heavy mix breaks in Hebrew), short lines set to wrap evenly so no word is left alone, and the app shows what it is (people and clothes) from the first second. Photos here are the licensed sample photos, only for the mockup.
