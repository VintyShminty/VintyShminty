VintyShminty, round 2

Cards, chips and menu

Three open questions from the component sheet review. Each section shows three options (or one proposal) in a 390px phone frame, using the Espresso and cream palette and Aileron. Nothing here is decided yet. Everything on this page is plain static content, so it also opens in Quick Look.

1. Item card2. Chips and badges3. Side drawer

1. Item card, three options

Every option carries the same required content: a portrait 3:4 photo, a heart with the favourite count, brand, size and condition, the seller price, the buyer price with a shield (buyer protection) and the states Sold, Bumped and a badge state. Sale and deal emphasis uses espresso, not red. Green is kept only for Sold, as in the decided palette.

Option A

Full-bleed and quiet

Inspired by a tall photo with no card box, square corners, a white circle heart at the bottom-right of the photo, a regular-weight two-line title below, then the price in bold (ASOS style). Adapted: state tags sit top-left as small rectangles, and the favourite count sits on the small line under the title.

Check

Contrast of every text and fill pair

Measured with the WCAG relative luminance formula, computed by a script from the same colours used above. Text needs 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text. All chip and badge labels here are small, so the 4.5:1 line applies to every pair. Chip outlines and fills are not relied on to find a control (the label does that), and the Option B outline is 5.1:1 anyway.

3. Navigation as a side drawer

Replaces the bottom tab bar. Light display weight against heavy emphasis for the menu items, small uppercase section labels, hairlines and generous spacing.

Proposal

Full-screen side drawer, no bottom bar

The five-tab bottom bar goes away. A menu button at the top left opens a drawer that fills the screen. Inbox and favourites stay in the top bar with small badges, so the alerts that matter stay visible without opening anything.
